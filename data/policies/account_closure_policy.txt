ACCOUNT CLOSURE AND DORMANCY POLICY — Cred Financial Services
Document ID: POL-ACCT-005
Effective Date: January 15, 2025
Version: 1.8

1. VOLUNTARY ACCOUNT CLOSURE
1.1 Customers may request account closure through the Cred app, by calling the helpline, or by visiting a branch.
1.2 Prerequisites for Closure:
    - All outstanding dues (credit card balance, loan EMIs, fees) must be fully settled.
    - Any pending disputes or chargebacks must be resolved.
    - Linked auto-pay mandates and standing instructions must be cancelled.
1.3 Processing Time: Account closure requests are processed within 7 working days after all prerequisites are met.
1.4 Closure Confirmation: A closure certificate is issued via email and post within 10 working days of closure.
1.5 Refund of Deposits: Any security deposit or credit balance remaining in the account will be refunded via NEFT/RTGS within 14 working days.

2. INVOLUNTARY ACCOUNT CLOSURE
2.1 Cred reserves the right to close an account if:
    - The customer provides fraudulent or misleading information during KYC or account opening.
    - The account is used for illegal activities, money laundering, or terrorism financing.
    - The customer violates the terms and conditions repeatedly.
    - Regulatory or law enforcement directives mandate account closure.
2.2 Notice: Cred will provide 30 days' written notice before involuntary closure (except in cases of fraud or legal mandates, where immediate closure may apply).

3. DORMANT / INACTIVE ACCOUNTS
3.1 Definition: An account with no customer-initiated transaction for 12 consecutive months is classified as "inactive." After 24 months of inactivity, it is classified as "dormant."
3.2 Restrictions on Dormant Accounts:
    - Debit transactions are blocked.
    - Credit transactions (incoming transfers) continue to be accepted.
    - The customer is notified via SMS and email at the 10-month and 22-month marks.
3.3 Reactivation: Dormant accounts can be reactivated by submitting an updated KYC and a written reactivation request. Reactivation is processed within 3 working days.

4. UNCLAIMED DEPOSITS
4.1 If an account remains dormant for 10 years, the balance is transferred to the Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (DEAF) as per RBI guidelines.
4.2 Customers may claim the transferred amount from the DEAF by submitting a claim to Cred, which will facilitate the refund from RBI.

5. DATA RETENTION POST-CLOSURE
5.1 Customer data, KYC records, and transaction history are retained for 5 years after account closure as required by PMLA 2002 and RBI Master Directions.
5.2 After the retention period, data is securely purged in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023.
