PERSONAL AND HOME LOAN POLICY — Cred Financial Services
Document ID: POL-LOAN-002
Effective Date: March 1, 2025
Version: 2.1

1. PERSONAL LOAN
1.1 Eligibility: Salaried individuals aged 23–58 years with a minimum monthly income of ₹25,000 and a CIBIL score of 700 or above.
1.2 Loan Amount: ₹50,000 to ₹25,00,000 based on income and credit profile.
1.3 Interest Rate: 10.5% – 18% per annum (reducing balance method). The exact rate is determined by the applicant's credit score and tenure.
1.4 Tenure: 12 to 60 months.
1.5 Processing Fee: 1.5% – 3% of the sanctioned loan amount (non-refundable).
1.6 Prepayment/Foreclosure: Permitted after 6 EMIs. A foreclosure charge of 4% of the outstanding principal applies for fixed-rate loans. No foreclosure charge on floating-rate loans.
1.7 Disbursement: Loan amount is disbursed within 48 hours of document verification and approval.

2. HOME LOAN
2.1 Eligibility: Salaried or self-employed individuals aged 25–65 years (at maturity). Minimum annual income of ₹5,00,000.
2.2 Loan Amount: ₹10,00,000 to ₹5,00,00,000. Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio is 80% for loans up to ₹30,00,000 and 75% for loans above ₹30,00,000.
2.3 Interest Rate: 8.5% – 11% per annum (floating rate, linked to repo rate).
2.4 Tenure: Up to 30 years, subject to the applicant retiring before the loan maturity date.
2.5 Processing Fee: 0.5% of the loan amount or ₹10,000, whichever is lower.
2.6 Required Documents: Identity proof, address proof, income proof (last 3 months salary slips + Form 16), property documents, and bank statements for the last 6 months.

3. EMI DEFAULT POLICY
3.1 Grace Period: A 7-day grace period is provided after the EMI due date.
3.2 Late Payment Charges: 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount.
3.3 Default Escalation:
    - 1 missed EMI: Reminder via SMS and email.
    - 2 consecutive missed EMIs: Phone call from the collections team + late fee.
    - 3 consecutive missed EMIs: Legal notice issued; account classified as NPA (Non-Performing Asset).
    - 6+ missed EMIs: Initiation of asset recovery / SARFAESI proceedings (for secured loans).
3.4 Restructuring: Borrowers facing genuine financial hardship may apply for EMI restructuring within 60 days of the first default. Subject to approval by the credit committee.

4. LOAN INSURANCE
4.1 Cred offers optional loan protection insurance covering death, permanent disability, and critical illness.
4.2 Premium: 0.5% of the outstanding loan amount per annum.
4.3 In the event of a valid claim, the outstanding loan balance is settled by the insurer.
