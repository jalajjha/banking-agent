CREDIT CARD POLICY — Cred Financial Services
Document ID: POL-CC-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Version: 3.2

1. CREDIT CARD ISSUANCE
1.1 Eligibility: Applicants must be at least 21 years of age, hold a valid government-issued ID, and maintain a minimum annual income of ₹3,00,000 (or equivalent).
1.2 Credit Limit Assignment: Initial credit limits are determined based on the applicant's credit score, income verification, and existing debt obligations. Limits range from ₹25,000 to ₹10,00,000.
1.3 Card Activation: Cards must be activated within 30 days of issuance via the Cred mobile app, registered phone number IVR, or by visiting a branch.

2. INTEREST RATES AND CHARGES
2.1 Annual Percentage Rate (APR): The standard APR is 36% per annum (3% per month) on revolving credit balances.
2.2 Cash Advance Fee: 2.5% of the transaction amount or ₹500, whichever is higher.
2.3 Foreign Transaction Fee: 3.5% of the transaction amount in INR equivalent.
2.4 Annual Fee: ₹499 for Classic cards, ₹1,999 for Gold cards, ₹4,999 for Platinum cards. First-year fee waiver applies for spending above ₹1,00,000 in the first 12 months.

3. LATE PAYMENT POLICY
3.1 Grace Period: A minimum payment of 5% of the outstanding balance (or ₹200, whichever is higher) must be made by the due date printed on the monthly statement. The grace period is 20 days from the statement generation date.
3.2 Late Payment Fee: If the minimum payment is not received by the due date, a late payment fee will be charged as follows:
    - Outstanding up to ₹10,000: ₹400
    - Outstanding ₹10,001 – ₹25,000: ₹750
    - Outstanding above ₹25,000: ₹1,100
3.3 Impact on Credit Score: Late payments exceeding 30 days will be reported to credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax) and may negatively impact the cardholder's credit score.
3.4 Repeated Defaults: Three consecutive missed payments will result in card suspension and potential account closure. The outstanding amount will be transferred to collections.

4. REWARD POINTS
4.1 Earning: Cardholders earn 2 reward points per ₹100 spent on online transactions and 1 point per ₹100 on offline transactions.
4.2 Redemption: Points can be redeemed for statement credits, merchandise, or travel bookings at a rate of 4 points = ₹1.
4.3 Expiry: Reward points expire 24 months from the date of earning if not redeemed.

5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
5.1 Unauthorized Transactions: Cardholders must report unauthorized transactions within 7 days of the transaction date. Cred will initiate an investigation within 2 business days.
5.2 Chargeback Process: Valid disputes are resolved within 45–90 days. During the investigation, the disputed amount is provisionally credited to the cardholder's account.
