KYC AND ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) POLICY — Cred Financial Services
Document ID: POL-KYC-003
Effective Date: April 15, 2025
Version: 4.0

1. KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) REQUIREMENTS
1.1 All customers must complete KYC verification before account activation or product enrollment.
1.2 Acceptable KYC Documents:
    - Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License.
    - Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, Utility Bill (not older than 3 months), Bank Statement, Passport.
    - Income Proof: Salary slips, IT Returns, Form 16, CA-certified financial statements (for self-employed).
1.3 Video KYC (V-KYC): Customers may complete KYC remotely via a video call with a Cred-authorized officer. V-KYC sessions are recorded and stored for 8 years as per RBI guidelines.
1.4 Re-KYC: Existing customers must update their KYC every 2 years (for high-risk profiles) or every 10 years (for low-risk profiles).

2. CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD)
2.1 Standard Due Diligence: Applied to all customers. Includes identity verification, address verification, and source of income assessment.
2.2 Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD): Applied to high-risk customers including:
    - Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and their relatives.
    - Customers from high-risk jurisdictions identified by FATF.
    - Customers with unusual or complex transaction patterns.
2.3 EDD measures include: senior management approval for account opening, enhanced monitoring of transactions, and annual review of the customer profile.

3. ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) CONTROLS
3.1 Transaction Monitoring: All customer transactions are monitored in real-time using rule-based and AI-driven systems. Alerts are generated for:
    - Cash transactions exceeding ₹10,00,000 in a single day.
    - Multiple transactions just below reporting thresholds (structuring).
    - Sudden spikes in transaction volume inconsistent with the customer's profile.
    - Transactions involving sanctioned entities or jurisdictions.
3.2 Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR): If a transaction is deemed suspicious after investigation, a Suspicious Transaction Report is filed with the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) within 7 days.
3.3 Cash Transaction Reporting (CTR): All cash transactions of ₹10,00,000 and above (or equivalent in foreign currency) are reported to FIU-IND monthly.

4. SANCTIONS SCREENING
4.1 All customers and counterparties are screened against UN, OFAC, EU, and India's UAPA sanctions lists at onboarding and periodically thereafter.
4.2 Matches trigger an immediate freeze on the account pending manual review and compliance officer approval.

5. RECORD RETENTION
5.1 All KYC documents, transaction records, and STR/CTR filings are retained for a minimum of 5 years after the cessation of the customer relationship, as mandated by PMLA 2002.
