DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL POLICY — Cred Financial Services
Document ID: POL-DISPUTE-004
Effective Date: February 1, 2025
Version: 2.5

1. SCOPE
1.1 This policy covers all disputes, complaints, and grievances raised by Cred customers regarding credit cards, loans, payments, account operations, and digital banking services.

2. COMPLAINT CHANNELS
2.1 Customers may raise complaints through the following channels:
    - Cred Mobile App: In-app support chat (available 24/7).
    - Email: support@credfinancial.com (response within 24 hours).
    - Phone: Toll-free helpline 1800-XXX-XXXX (available Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 8 PM IST).
    - Branch Visit: Walk-in to any Cred branch during business hours.
    - Written Complaint: Postal mail to the Registered Office address.
2.2 All complaints are assigned a unique Ticket ID for tracking purposes.

3. RESOLUTION TIMELINES
3.1 Level 1 — Customer Service Team:
    - Acknowledgment: Within 24 hours of complaint receipt.
    - Resolution: Within 7 working days for standard complaints.
    - Complex cases (involving third parties, cross-border transactions): Within 30 working days.
3.2 Level 2 — Nodal Officer:
    - If the customer is unsatisfied with the Level 1 resolution, they may escalate to the Nodal Officer within 30 days.
    - The Nodal Officer will review and respond within 15 working days.
3.3 Level 3 — Banking Ombudsman:
    - If the complaint remains unresolved after Level 2, the customer may approach the RBI Banking Ombudsman under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.
    - Cred will fully cooperate with the Ombudsman's investigation.

4. TRANSACTION DISPUTE PROCESS
4.1 Unauthorized Transaction Claims:
    - Must be reported within 7 days of the transaction.
    - If reported within 3 days, the customer's liability is zero (as per RBI circular on limiting liability).
    - If reported between 4–7 days, liability is capped at ₹10,000.
    - If reported after 7 days, liability is determined on a case-by-case basis.
4.2 Merchant Disputes:
    - Cred initiates a chargeback with the acquiring bank / payment network.
    - Provisional credit is issued within 10 working days.
    - Final resolution within 45–90 days depending on the card network (Visa/Mastercard/RuPay).

5. REFUND POLICY
5.1 Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days.
5.2 Refund method: Original payment method (card credit, bank transfer, or wallet credit).
5.3 Interest reversal: If a disputed charge accrued interest before resolution, the interest is reversed upon approval of the dispute.

6. COMPENSATION
6.1 If Cred fails to resolve a complaint within the stipulated timeline, the customer is entitled to compensation of ₹100 per day of delay, up to a maximum of ₹5,000.
